# Forks and derivative systems

CHAOSOS is open for independent forks and derivative projects under the Apache
License 2.0.

## Naming freedom

A derivative may have its own product name, interface, feature set, release
schedule, and visual identity. It does not have to use `CHAOSOS` as its primary
name.

## Preserve source attribution

When distributing a derivative, preserve the applicable copyright, license,
and attribution notices, including the readable attribution from `NOTICE`, as
required by Apache License 2.0 section 4. A recommended user-facing description
is:

> **[Derivative System Name] — based on CHAOSOS**

The attribution may appear in the derivative's About page, documentation, or a
notice file. Include a change notice for modified files where required by the
license.

## Do not imply official status

Do not describe a fork as an official CHAOSOS release, do not use the original
project identity in a way that suggests endorsement, and do not publish a
modified build under the original release number. Use a distinct version name
and make the fork's maintainer clear.

## Contribution and licensing

Forking and submitting a contribution are different actions. Contributions
submitted for inclusion in the original project are governed by the repository
license unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. Contributors must
have the rights needed to submit their work and must identify third-party code
or assets they include.
