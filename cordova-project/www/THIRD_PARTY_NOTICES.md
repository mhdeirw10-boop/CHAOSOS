# Third-party notices and asset review

This release package does not intentionally bundle third-party JavaScript
frameworks or package-manager dependencies in the web runtime. The Cordova
wrapper declares Cordova and its Android platform/plugins in
`cordova-project/package.json` and `cordova-project/config.xml`; their own
licenses apply.

The repository contains project assets under `assets/`. Before commercial
redistribution or publication of a new binary, the maintainer should verify the
origin and license of every image, font, sound, icon, and other non-original
asset and add the relevant notices here. Do not assume that the repository-wide
Apache license overrides a third-party asset license.

This file is a release-preparation inventory, not a legal certification that
all historical assets or prior contributions have been cleared. Unknown or
unverified assets should be replaced with original or properly licensed assets
before a commercial release.
