CHAOSOS v3.1.5 EXPERIMENTAL — NATURAL VOCABULARY AND SECRET PERSONA

New word modules under js/brain/words/:
- courtesy.js: thanks, apologies, acknowledgements and farewells
- emotions.js: common emotional statements
- technology.js: everyday technology vocabulary
- philosophy.js: questions about truth, consciousness and limits
- naturalConversation.js: conservative full-phrase matching for conversational variants
- secrets.js: in-character response to attempts to reveal CHAOSAI's fictional secrets, plus a separate privacy notice when a user says they have a personal secret

registry.js now exposes resolveHandlers() for pattern-driven modules and more consistent Arabic normalization. app.js dispatches dynamic handlers and dictionary/pattern matching before the broad local language runtime and knowledge fallback, in both the direct response and orchestrated conversation paths. commands.js remains the command-only dispatcher.

Privacy and security note: secrets.js does not write user-shared secrets to storage and is not an access-control mechanism. The app may retain conversation content according to its own session/memory features; users should not enter passwords, tokens or highly sensitive information.

Stable baseline remains eye_of_chaos_voidos_v3_0_0. No APK or physical device test is claimed.
