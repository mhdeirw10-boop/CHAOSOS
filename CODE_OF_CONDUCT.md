# Community guidelines

CHAOSOS welcomes technical disagreement, direct criticism, bug reports, and
strongly worded reviews. The goal is to understand problems and improve the
system, not to require praise or a particular writing style.

Expected behaviour:

- Focus criticism on behaviour, design, implementation, documentation, or
  project decisions.
- Provide reproducible evidence and proposed corrections when possible.
- Respect other contributors and do not target people with threats, harassment,
  discrimination, doxxing, or repeated personal abuse.
- Do not publish passwords, private keys, personal data, or exploitable
  vulnerability details in public discussions.

Maintainers may moderate content that exposes private information, creates a
security risk, or targets people rather than the work. Technical criticism,
including negative assessments of the project, must not be removed merely
because it is negative.
