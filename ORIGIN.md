# Project origin and identity

CHAOSOS is an independent software project within the Eye of Chaos project.
Its creator and owner is Void Walker.

The project is developed in an Arab League member state whose identity is
intentionally not disclosed by the creator. The project uses a global,
mysterious visual identity and supports Arabic as one of its interface
languages.

CHAOSOS is not an official project of the League of Arab States, is not
administered by that organization, and does not claim its support or endorsement.
